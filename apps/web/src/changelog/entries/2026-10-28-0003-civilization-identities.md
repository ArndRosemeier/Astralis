# Know your civilization

- See every racial trait and its effects during expedition setup and in your civilization profile.
- Revised trait prices charge more for stronger tiers and powerful habitat abilities. All 26 civilizations now spend exactly 10 net points, with their costs and drawback refunds visible.
- Civilization kits focus on their identities; shared Electrosense is no longer a filler pick. Saved custom designs can be revised in the Species lab if their new cost exceeds the budget.
- Compare civilizations side by side, including combined industry, research and commerce bonuses, unique traits and home environments.
- Amphibious aquatic civilizations now settle dry land without the living-medium penalty, as their trait promises. Atmosphere, gravity and other environmental hazards still apply.
