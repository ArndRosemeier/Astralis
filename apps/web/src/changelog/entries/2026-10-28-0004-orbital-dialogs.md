# A little light around your decisions

- The title screen now opens against one of four colorful nebulae, chosen randomly at startup, with brighter stars behind the live 3D world.
- Every dialog now has a slowly orbiting light along its edge and a soft cyan-and-violet Nebula shadow beneath it.
- The frame stays in place while long dialogs scroll, including expedition setup.
- Turning off interface animation or enabling reduced motion keeps the border still.
