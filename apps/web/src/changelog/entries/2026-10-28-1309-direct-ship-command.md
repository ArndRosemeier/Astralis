# Point your ships toward the stars

- Your spacecraft now appear as rotating 3D models around their star on the galaxy map. Click a ship, Shift-click to add or remove others, then click a destination to queue the move and clear selection.
- Groups can include individual ships from several fleets. Partial fleets detach automatically; invalid destinations explain why and keep the group selected. Touch screens have an Add ships toggle.
- Matching ship models now appear in fleet lists, the ship designer and battle replays. Spacecraft icons replace maritime ship symbols.
- Fixed a title resize error when entering games or online rooms. Test-battle replays now respect reduced motion.
- Phone maps keep ships above the system panel. Reduced-motion buttons no longer jitter under the pointer. Saves from build 0.010 remain compatible.
