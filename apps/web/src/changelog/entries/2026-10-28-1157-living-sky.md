# A more living sky

- The sharp discovery ding is now an ethereal, slowly blooming harmony with a gentle airy tail. It accompanies new expeditions, discoveries and title interactions.
- Opening expedition setup no longer repeats the discovery sound before launch.
- Visiting ships now take varied approaches, with different speeds, banks, sizes and pauses between visits. Shuttles choose fresh travel and docking times each circuit.
- The ASTRALIS sculpture occasionally leaves its place to orbit behind the world and return. The orbit button can send it around immediately.
- A randomly chosen sun glows in the upper-left corner, with a gently flowing corona. Its appearance stays consistent throughout the session.
