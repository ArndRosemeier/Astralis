# Beyond the Veil

- Begin an expedition with twelve biologically distinct civilizations among real nearby stars.
- Build colonies, choose research, design vessels and prepare fleet doctrines.
- Watch deterministic 3D engagements and inspect their battle reports.
- Pursue conquest, the Concord, the Gardeners’ Legacy, the Approach or the score victory.
- Invite friends into private GameHub expeditions with host migration and seat recovery.
- Save automatically, export portable games and explore the searchable field manual.
- Enjoy original space art, species portraits, ambient music and cinematic effects.
