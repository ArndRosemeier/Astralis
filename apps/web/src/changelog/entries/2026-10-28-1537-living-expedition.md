# An expedition you can see and feel

- Founding a colony now brings a 3D landing: the ship descends, habitats unfold and a world lights up. Surveys reveal orbiting worlds, research assembles field-specific inventions, and new ships leave an orbital yard.
- Construction, population milestones, terraforming, first contact, treaties, artifacts, leaders, repairs, treasury milestones and victories now receive visual feedback. Losses have their own quieter treatment.
- Accomplishments illuminate their locations on the galaxy map. A highlights strip and the turn report let you replay moments from this session without interrupting your orders.
- Worlds keep visible cities and completed facilities as they grow. Research lights an expanding constellation of discoveries, and resource counters show their changes.
- Major moments play one short, skippable scene per turn. Routine gains use smaller signals. Pause, replay, disable automatic highlights, or use reduced motion; the game also remains playable without WebGL.
- Highlights follow committed turns, never unconfirmed or cancelled orders. Existing saves from builds 0.010–0.012 remain compatible.
