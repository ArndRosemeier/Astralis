# No wasted turns

- Choose an eligible research project before ending a turn, or delegate research to your advisor.
- Empty colony queues trigger a review with direct links to each colony and the option to produce trade goods.
- Trade goods convert two unused industry into one credit, after synthetic assembly. Production still requires stability of at least 25.
- Colony income and the field manual explain trade goods; research opens at a relevant tier.
- Multiplayer submissions and expired timers respect the research requirement.
- Existing 0.015 saves remain compatible.
