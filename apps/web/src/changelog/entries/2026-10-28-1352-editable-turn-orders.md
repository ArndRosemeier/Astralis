# Your turn, your orders

- All turn orders remain editable until End turn. Open Turn orders to cancel individual actions or the entire draft, with undo and redo. Autosaves, manual saves and exports preserve the editable draft.
- Ships with planned routes stay individually visible at their current location. Amber arrows and route lines show planned departures; traveling ships use cyan indicators.
- Select ships again to redirect them. Click their current star to cancel their planned moves and deselect them. Ships already traveling can change course from their actual position, without jumping back to their previous star.
- Ship click areas no longer obscure destination stars, and queued fleets no longer overlap into one clump.
- Builds 0.010 and 0.011 remain loadable.
