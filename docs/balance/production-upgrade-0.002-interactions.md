# Production special interactions

These three combinations were selected from the largest interactions of the first diagnostic research run. They are measured with the actual engine, both seats and up to twelve independent seeds. Positive residual means the pair adds more terminal score than the sum of its isolated effects. An interval wholly above +5 or below −5 percentage points is flagged.

- subterranean + hive-mind: +19.90% residual (+6.74% … +33.06%), 12 paired seeds; flagged: true.
- spore-borne + hive-mind: -4.31% residual (-11.56% … +2.95%), 12 paired seeds; flagged: false.
- terraformers + hive-mind: +0.89% residual (-1.30% … +3.08%), 12 paired seeds; flagged: false.
