# Astronomy data attribution and licence

The derived JSON catalogue files in `src/` are distributed under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International** because they incorporate HYG data. This licence covers these astronomy data files, not the surrounding game code or original generated artwork.

Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

- **HYG Database v4.1**, David Nash / Astronexus. Source: https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database/tree/main/hyg/CURRENT . Original licence: CC BY-SA 4.0. Astralis selects, groups, rotates and augments records; it does not redistribute an unchanged original catalogue.
- **The 10 parsec sample in the Gaia era, v2**, Reylé, Jardine, Fouqué, Caballero, Smart and Sozzetti. Source: https://dc.g-vo.org/tableinfo/tenpc.main ; publication: https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202140985 . Used for measured positions, multiplicity, names and spectral types.
- **The Fifth Catalogue of Nearby Stars: Continuously Updated Version**, Golovin, Reffert, Just, Jordan, Vani and Jahreiß. Source: https://dc.g-vo.org/tableinfo/cns5update.main ; publication: https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202244250 . Includes astrometry from Gaia DR3, Hipparcos, Spitzer and ground-based observations.
- **NASA Exoplanet Archive, Planetary Systems Composite Parameters (PSCompPars)**. Data service: https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/TAP/sync ; documentation: https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/API_PS_columns.html . Operated by Caltech under contract with NASA through the Exoplanet Exploration Program. Its composite measurements can originate from different publications and are not necessarily a self-consistent orbital solution.
- **Pecaut & Mamajek (2013, ApJS 208, 9), updated dwarf sequence 2022.04.16**, Eric Mamajek. https://www.pas.rochester.edu/~emamajek/EEM_dwarf_UBVIJHK_colors_Teff.txt . Used for clearly labelled model estimates and V-band-to-bolometric correction, rather than presenting missing stellar measurements as known facts.
- **NASA NSSDC Planetary Fact Sheet**: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/ . Nominal Solar System values.

The app's credits should link to this notice and retain attribution when redistributing the catalogue. Scientific publications should consult the original references and uncertainty columns; game estimates are not a substitute for those measurements.
